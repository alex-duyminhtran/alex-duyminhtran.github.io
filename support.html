Good to know

A few common questions.

Can I record more than one feeling a day?

Yes. Each save creates a separate moment. Choose a feeling, keep or adjust its strength, and add a note or activities if you want to. After saving, use Add another to return to the form.

Several moments can belong to the same day. They appear in Check-in for that day and in your Journal.

How do I add a moment from a past day?

In Check-in, tap the date button with the calendar icon near the top of the screen. Choose a day, then tap Use this day. Your unfinished feeling, strength, note, and activities stay in the form when you change the day.

Future dates are unavailable. Past-day entries show Time not recorded because Moodumi does not invent a time for a moment added later.

Can I change or delete a saved moment?

Yes. Open the moment’s menu in Check-in or Journal and choose the edit or delete action. Editing lets you update the moment; cancelling leaves the saved version unchanged. Deletion asks for confirmation.

A change to a moment can also change the Insights or Growth reflection based on it.

What does feeling strength mean?

Strength describes how strongly you felt the feeling you selected, on a scale from 1 to 5. It is not a score for how good your day was. A stronger feeling is not automatically a better or worse mood.

You can save a moment with the default strength of 3, or adjust it to fit your experience.

Where is my journal stored? Does it sync?

Moodumi stores your moments and Growth records locally on your device. There is no account, and Moodumi does not provide syncing between devices.

Apple may include app data in your device backups or device-to-device transfer, depending on your settings. Those features are separate from Moodumi. Read the storage and backups section of the Privacy Policy for more detail.

Can support recover missing or deleted entries?

The developer cannot retrieve locally stored entries. Moodumi does not hold a server copy or an account backup.

Any recovery through an Apple device backup depends on the backup that exists and Apple’s restore process; it is not guaranteed. Before deleting the app or changing devices, review your backup settings. Offloading the app and deleting the app can affect local data differently.

How do Insights work?

Insights summarises recorded moments across daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly views. You can explore feeling frequency, strength within a feeling, and recorded-day coverage.

A day without entries is unknown, not a good or bad day. Several moments on one day count as one recorded day.

Where do Growth reflections come from?

Growth looks at recorded feelings and activity tags from the last seven days, including today. It needs at least three matching moments across three different days before presenting a pattern. The nonspecific Other activity is excluded from patterns.

Tap View supporting moments to see the entries behind a reflection, then decide whether it fits your experience. With less history, Moodumi offers a general reflection prompt instead.

These calculations happen on your device. Your notes are not interpreted by an AI service, and a repeated activity does not prove that it caused a feeling.

What are the gentle practices?

Growth offers three short, optional practices: A quiet pause, A kind sentence, and One manageable step.

Open a practice to read its instructions. Tapping I tried it records an attempt, and you can optionally say whether it helped. Closing the instructions without tapping that button creates no completion record.

From your recent steps, you can update feedback, repeat a practice, or delete an attempt.

Which devices can use Moodumi? Do I need an account?

Moodumi is designed for iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 and later. No account is needed. The App Store release is coming soon.
