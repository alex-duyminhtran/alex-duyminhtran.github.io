Privacy Policy

A clear look at what stays in the app, what happens when you email us, and how this website is hosted.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version: Moodumi stores your journal and Growth records locally on your device. There is no account, and the app has no advertising or analytics services. Your device backups, this website, and any email you choose to send are explained below.

About this policy

Moodumi is developed by Minh Tran. This policy applies to the Moodumi app for iPhone and iPad and this Moodumi website.

For privacy questions, email alex.duyminhtran@gmail.com.

Information in the app

Moodumi saves the information you choose to record, including:

  • Feelings, feeling strength, optional notes, activity tags, and journal dates and recorded times when available.
  • Your optional responses to Growth reflections.
  • Practice attempts and any helpfulness feedback you choose to add.

This information is used on your device to display your Journal, calculate Insights, show Growth reflections and their supporting moments, and keep your practice history.

The app does not require an account and does not send your journal or Growth records to the developer. It does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Reflections are calculated on your device from recorded feelings and activities; your notes are not sent to an AI service.

Storage and backups

Your records are stored in the app’s local database on your device. Moodumi does not provide its own server storage, account recovery, or syncing between devices.

Depending on your Apple device settings, app data may be included in an iCloud or computer backup, or transferred when you move to another device. These features are managed by Apple and your backup settings. Local storage does not mean that your data can never be included in a device backup.

You can review backup settings on your device. For details about Apple’s handling of information, read Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Your choices and deletion

Notes, activities, reflection responses, and practice feedback are optional. You can use Moodumi without creating an account.

You can edit or delete individual moments in the app. You can also update practice feedback or delete individual practice attempts from your practice history.

Deleting the app using your device’s Delete App option removes its active local app data. Offloading an app may retain its documents and data. Deleting a record or the app does not necessarily remove copies already held in device backups; manage those separately through Apple’s backup controls.

The developer cannot view, retrieve, or delete records stored only on your device. There is no account or developer-held copy from which to restore them.

When you contact support

If you email alex.duyminhtran@gmail.com, Minh Tran receives your email address, message, and any files you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request and resolve the issue you raise.

Support correspondence is handled through the email provider and retained as needed to address your request and any applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive journal notes or screenshots containing personal details. You can ask about deletion of support correspondence by using the same email address.

Contacting support is voluntary and does not give the developer access to your app’s local database.

This website

This website uses GitHub Pages for hosting. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, contact forms, or external fonts to this website. Following an external link or opening your email app takes you to a service governed by its own privacy practices.

Policy updates and contact
